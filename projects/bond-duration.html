Objective

I started this project to build a practical understanding of interest-rate risk: how much bond prices move when rates change, and how that sensitivity differs across Treasuries and corporate bonds. Duration is the core measure I am using to study it.

Work completed so far

  • Set up an Excel sheet covering Treasuries and corporate bonds.
  • Calculated duration for each bond.
  • Applied simple +100 and −100 basis-point interest-rate shocks to estimate price changes.

Analysis workflow

The analysis follows four steps, from the bonds in the sheet to an estimated price change under each rate scenario.

Tools

  • Microsoft Excel for the bond sheet, duration calculations, and rate scenarios.
  • Concepts: bond duration, basis-point rate shocks, and interest-rate sensitivity.

Current status and next steps

In progress. The duration calculations and ±100-basis-point scenarios for Treasuries and corporate bonds are set up. The broader project is unfinished: planned extensions to mortgage-backed securities (MBS) and credit stress testing were not completed.

Next, I plan to finalize and document the Treasury and corporate-bond analysis, then add its methodology and results to this page.
